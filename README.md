# PAWVERSE – Where Every Paw Finds Love 🐾❤️✨

> **Core Message:** *“Animals cannot express their needs in words, but they can express them through their actions. Give them care, patience, kindness, and unconditional love.”*

**PAWVERSE** is an immersive, interactive 3D virtual pet care and emotional bonding experience built with **A-Frame WebXR**, featuring procedural 3D pet models, realistic care mechanics, positive-reinforcement trick training, autonomous pet AI, and a procedural Web Audio synthesizer.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

### 1. 🏠 3D Pet Adoption Centre
- Choose from 8 diverse animal breeds, each with distinct procedural coloration, ear shapes, tail types, and personality traits:
  - **Golden Retriever** (Friendly, loyal, playful)
  - **German Shepherd** (Alert, protective, brave)
  - **Labrador Retriever** (Playful fetch champion)
  - **Siberian Husky** (Adventurous, vocal, blue eyes)
  - **Persian Cat** (Gentle, regal, purring)
  - **Orange Tabby Cat** (Curious, agile acrobat)
  - **Holland Lop Rabbit** (Gentle, twitchy nose, puff tail)
  - **Shiba Inu** (Proud, intelligent, cheerful smile)
- Give your companion a custom name and welcome them into your virtual home.

### 2. 💖 Dynamic Emotional Bonding System
- **Bonding Tiers**: *New Acquaintance* ➡️ *Warm Friend* ➡️ *Close Companion* ➡️ *Best Friend Forever* ➡️ *Eternal Soulmate*.
- **Autonomous AI & Proximity**: Pets recognize positive interactions, wag their tails faster when happy, and naturally trot over to stay close to you as the emotional bond deepens.
- **Gentle Feedback**: Never harsh or punitive—prompts guide you with empathy when your pet feels hungry, sleepy, or in need of care.

### 3. 🛁 Interactive 3D Pet Care & Activities
- **Feeding Station**: Fill the bowl with nutritious kibble; watch your pet walk over, dip their head, and happily crunch their meal.
- **Fresh Water Fountain**: Maintain hydration with clean, refreshing water.
- **Warm Bubble Bath & Spa**: Wash off dirt in the tub with bubbly foam and water droplet particles.
- **Fur Grooming**: Brush fur smooth to improve cleanliness and trigger soothing purrs.
- **Cozy Pet Bed**: Rest and recharge energy with sleeping animations and gentle floating *Zzz* particles.
- **Veterinary Corner**: Perform routine checkups on the medical exam table with a digital health scanner to keep your pet at 100% health.

### 4. 🎾 Interactive Playground & Trick Academy
- **Fetch the Ball**: Throw the tennis ball across the lawn in a parabolic trajectory. Your pet detects the bounce, sprints to retrieve the ball, picks it up, and trots proudly back to drop it at your feet!
- **Trick Academy**: Practice positive reinforcement commands:
  1. *"Sit!"* — Sits attentively on haunches.
  2. *"Lie Down!"* — Rests paws forward in peace.
  3. *"Spin!"* — Pirouettes 360° with excitement.
  4. *"Roll Over!"* — Playfully rolls over tummy.
  5. *"Speak / Bark!"* — Greets you with a joyful yip/meow.

### 5. 🏆 Progression, Care Badges & Wardrobe
- Earn **Love Points** through consistent, attentive care.
- Unlock 8 distinctive **Care Badges** (*Master Chef*, *Splish Splash*, *Fetch Champion*, *Trick Academy Master*, *Eternal Soulmate*).
- Equip charming 3D accessories in real-time (*Ruby Bowtie*, *Golden Bell Collar*, *Party Hat*, *Cool Aviator Shades*).

## 🎮 Controls & Interaction Guide

### 🥽 Meta Quest 3S VR Controls
- **Left Thumbstick**: Smooth locomotion walking around the home & playground.
- **Right Thumbstick**: Smooth turning.
- **Trigger Button**: Click 3D care stations, pet companion, select items, and throw the ball.
- **Laser Raycasters**: Interactive beams on both hands for pointing and selecting.
- **In-World 3D VR Board**: View live pet stats and trigger care actions directly inside VR without needing a 2D screen!

### 💻 Windows Desktop PC Controls
- **W / A / S / D**: Walk around the environment.
- **Mouse Drag**: Look around 360°.
- **Left Mouse Click / [E]**: Interact with items and pet your animal.
- **[F]**: Feed pet bowl.
- **[G]**: Pet and stroke companion.
- **[Space]**: Throw tennis ball for fetch.
- **[1 - 5]**: Direct trick commands (*Sit, Down, Spin, Roll, Speak*).
- **[ESC]**: Close active menus/modals.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

- **Single Standalone File**: 100% self-contained in `index.html` (all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WebXR components).
- **Procedural 3D Pet Rig (`procedural-pet`)**: Built directly with Three.js geometries and materials (torso, articulated head, snout, ears, 4 walking limbs, wagging tail, accessories) with 60–90 FPS performance on Meta Quest 3S.
- **Web Audio API Procedural Synthesizer**: No external MP3/WAV files required—procedurally generates harmonic ambient chords, dog barks, cat meows, rabbit chirps, crunching kibble, splashing water, brushing sweeps, and fanfare chimes.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Open `index.html` in any modern WebXR-enabled browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Meta Quest Browser).
2. For desktop PC, click **"Enter Pawverse (Desktop 3D)"**.
3. For Meta Quest 3S headset, click **"Meta Quest 3S VR Mode"** or click the VR goggles icon at the bottom right.
